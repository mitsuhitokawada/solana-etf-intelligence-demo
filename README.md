## About

### Too many tabs, too little clarity

Crypto investors check ETF flows, SOL price and on-chain data in different places. It is slow and easy to misread.

- ETF flows, price and on-chain data live on separate sites
- Hard to see if money, price and usage move together
- Investors need one fast, clear view
- Our idea: a pro-style dashboard on a single screen

### How it works

Pick a period, and the whole screen updates. ETF flows lead the page, with price, on-chain data and AI Insight beside them.

- Switch between 7D, 30D and 90D with one tap
- Big net inflow number and a daily bar chart for ETF flows
- SOL price chart and on-chain cards: addresses, transactions, DEX volume
- AI Insight card: three short lines for each period

### Why it matters and what's next

One clear screen helps investors read the market faster. Today the app runs on demo data, and every number says so.

- Faster reading of ETF flows, price and usage together
- Dark terminal style with monospace numbers and thin lines
- Every number is marked DEMO DATA, so nothing is misleading
- Next step: connect real data sources
